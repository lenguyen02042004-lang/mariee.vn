## I. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & VALUE PROPOSITION

### 1. The Core Vision
**1Hub** is not a social network; it is a **Living Heritage Ecosystem**. It is designed to be the world's first cross-industry hub where professional excellence is verified through execution, not just presentation. We shift the focus from the individual ("Me") to the collective achievement ("Us").

### 2. Identifying the Industry Gaps (The "Why")
Current platforms suffer from significant "Gaps" that 1Hub is engineered to bridge:
*   **The Silence Gap:** Designers, Architects, and Developers work in silos. 1Hub integrates them into a single **Project Canvas**.
*   **The Verification Gap:** Portfolios on Behance or LinkedIn are easily forged. 1Hub uses **Peer-to-Peer Cross-Verification** to ensure every "Credit" is authentic.
*   **The Depth Gap:** Most platforms prioritize "pretty pictures." 1Hub prioritizes **Case Studies**—the intellectual logic behind the solution.
*   **The Middleman Gap:** Freelance sites focus on low-cost transactions. 1Hub focuses on **Premium Value Synergy** and professional networking without transaction fees.

## II. THE MASTERY SYSTEM (HIERARCHY & RANKING)

To achieve international prestige, 1Hub employs a **Reverse Hierarchy System** (Level 1 is the highest). Rank is a "Proof of Merit."

### 1. Structure: 7 Levels | 3 Classes

#### **Class I: THE ELITES (Global Authority)**
*   **Level 1: THE MASTER** – The ultimate authority. Their vote can redefine industry standards. Reaching L1 requires endorsement from other L1s and a legacy of high-impact Case Studies.
*   **Level 2: THE MENTOR** – High-reputation experts who focus on nurturing the ecosystem through high-weight voting and professional guidance.

#### **Class II: THE LEADERS (Strategic Execution)**
*   **Level 3: THE EXPERT** – Professionals whose Case Studies serve as "Textbooks" for the community.
*   **Level 4: THE PROJECT LEAD** – Capable of initiating complex multi-author projects and curating top-tier teams.

#### **Class III: THE EXECUTORS (Active Contributors)**
*   **Level 5: THE SPECIALIST** – Proven professionals with a steady stream of verified contributions.
*   **Level 6: THE COLLABORATOR** – Individuals with at least two cross-verified projects.
*   **Level 7: THE CREATOR** – The entry-point for verified professionals building their initial Trust-Score.

### 2. The "Onboarding Protocol" for Global Experts
We recognize that world-class experts will not start at Level 7.
*   **The Alpha Board Review:** During the launch phase, established global experts can apply for an **"Early Master"** status. 
*   **Proof of Excellence:** Applicants submit existing evidence (GitHub, physical architecture projects, industry awards).
*   **Standardized Entry:** Post-launch, all entries are L7, but rapid promotion is possible through high-weight votes from Class I members.

## III. CORE CONTENT ARCHITECTURE (THE TRINITY)

The logic of 1Hub rests on three interconnected entities:

### 1. Personal Portfolio (Professional Identity)
Unlike a static CV, this is a **Trust-Score Dashboard**.
*   **Network Graph:** A visual map showing who you have worked with and their respective levels.
*   **Skill Heatmap:** Generated from verified Case Studies, not self-claimed tags.
*   **Contribution Pulse:** Shows the frequency and impact of your work over time.

### 2. Project Portfolio (Collaboration Hub)
This is the "Container" for the work.
*   **Multi-Player Authorship:** A project belongs to a team, not an individual.
*   **Contribution Timeline (The Project Pulse):** A dynamic chart showing:
    *   **The Founder:** Who started the vision.
    *   **The Rescuer:** Who stepped in to fix critical issues.
    *   **The Core:** The heavy lifters throughout the timeline.
*   **Status:** "In-Progress" (Seeking partners via AI Matchmaking) or "Completed" (Heritage mode).

### 3. Case Study (Intellectual Asset)
The most granular and valuable unit of 1Hub.
*   **The "How" over the "What":** Every Case Study must include:
    *   *Context:* The problem.
    *   *Solution Logic:* The technical or creative process.
    *   *Bridge (The Synergy):* How you interacted with other disciplines (e.g., How the Dev optimized the Designer's animation).
    *   *Legacy:* Key takeaways or shared resources for the community.

## IV. THE TRUST ENGINE (VOTING & REPUTATION)

### 1. Weighted Voting Logic
A vote on 1Hub is a **Professional Endorsement**.
*   **Level Weight:** A vote from an **L1 Master** carries significantly more weight ($W=10$) than a vote from an **L7 Creator** ($W=1$).
*   **Reciprocity Ban:** AI monitors "Vote-for-Vote" patterns. If two users vote for each other without a shared verified project, the weight is nullified to prevent "Reputation Farming."

### 2. Cross-Verification (The Social Proof)
*   No project appears on a profile unless the **Project Lead** tags the member and the member **Accepts** the specific role and timeline contribution.
*   This creates an immutable chain of professional history.
