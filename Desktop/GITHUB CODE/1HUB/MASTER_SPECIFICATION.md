## II. THE ATOMIC UNIT: THE TRINITY OF VALUE

The ecosystem is built on three interconnected data entities:

### 1. Professional Identity (The Portfolio)
*   **Concept:** A "Trust-Score Dashboard" rather than a CV.
*   **Core Metrics:** 
    *   **Verified Merit Score ($R$):** Real-time reputation weight.

### 2. Collaboration Hub (The Project Portfolio)
*   **Concept:** A shared container for collective work.
*   **Multi-Author Ownership:** Projects belong to teams. Every credit is verified by the **Project Owner** and accepted by the **Contributor**.
*   **Contribution Timeline (The Project Pulse):** A vertical/horizontal timeline tracking roles (Founder, Core, Rescuer) and duration of involvement.

### 3. Intellectual Asset (The Case Study)
*   **Concept:** The "Knowledge Core" of the project.
*   **The Structural Requirement:**
    *   **Context:** The challenge/problem statement.
    *   **Process ("The How"):** The technical/creative logic behind the solution.
    *   **Multimedia Blocks:**
        *   **Logic Blocks:** Flowcharts, code snippets, or mathematical models.
        *   **Visual Proofs:** High-fidelity images with technical metadata.
    *   **Collaborative Bridge:** How cross-industry synergy was achieved.
    *   **Legacy Assets:** Shared documentation, snippets, or blueprints.
    *   **IP Privacy Layer (Partial Visibility):** Option to disclose the "Strategic Logic" (The How) publicly while restricting access to "Core Technical Assets" (The Secret) to protect proprietary intellectual property.
    *   **Structural Gatekeeper:** Minimum 300 words required for publication to ensure depth.

## V. THE REPUTATION ALGORITHM & TRUST STANDARDS

### 1. Weighted Reputation Score ($R$)
The score of an expert is a dynamic function of community recognition:
$$R = \sum (V \times W \times M) \times C \times T$$
*   **$V$ (Vote):** Endorsement from a peer.
*   **$W$ (Weight):** The Level of the voter (L1 = 10x impact).
*   **$M$ (Impact Multiplier):** **The Rescuer Multiplier** ($2\times$ weight) is applied to those who join critical-path projects to resolve high-risk issues.
*   **$C$ (Consistency):** Frequency of verified activity.
*   **$T$ (Time Decay):** Ensuring current expertise outweighs historical achievements.

### 2. Global Trust Standards & Transparency (Audit Log)
To maintain international credibility, 1Hub implements a **Decentralized Audit System**:
*   **Level-up Transparency:** Every promotion to L1/L2 is documented in a public **Audit Log**, detailing the endorsing peers and verified Case Studies.
*   **Reciprocity Ban:** Automated detection of "Vote-Rings" to nullify artificial reputation farming.

### 2. Dispute Resolution: The Record-Only Philosophy (KISS)
1Hub acts as an **Objective Witness**, not a Judge. To keep the system lightweight and professional:
*   **Parallel Data Streams:** If the Project Owner and a Member provide conflicting contribution data, the system **does not block** the project. Instead, it displays both versions of the truth side-by-side.
*   **The Dispute Note:** Members have the right to attach a short "Dispute Note" (max 200 words) directly to their timeline entry if they disagree with the recorded contribution.
*   **Community Audit:** Discrepancies are visible to all users. The "public misalignment" serves as a natural deterrent for dishonesty, pushing the responsibility of integrity back to the individuals.

## XIII. SOVEREIGN GOVERNANCE PROTOCOL (THE RULES OF POWER)

To ensure the integrity of the 1HUB ecosystem, all participants must adhere to the **Sovereign Governance Protocol**. This defines the relationship between identity, authorship, and authority.

### 1. Project Hierarchy (Roles & Permissions)
Roles are assigned based on **Heritage Responsibility**, not just titles:
*   **Project Owner (Chủ dự án):** The architect of the project "container" on 1HUB.
    *   **Power:** Invite members, assign initial roles, and validate the primary Timeline.
    *   **Responsibility:** Ultimate accountability for the overall project manifest.
*   **Leader / Co-Leader:** Authorized delegates of the Owner.
    *   **Power:** Edit project descriptions, approve member Case Studies, and moderate Expert Insights.
*   **Contributor (Thành viên):** The active executors of the work.
    *   **Power:** Author their own "Intellectual Asset" (Case Study) and claim their specific timeline span.
    *   **Sovereign Right:** The right to **"Dispute" (Kháng nghị)** if the Owner incorrectly records their contribution period.

### 2. Authorship & Content Lifecycle
*   **The Manifest (Strategic Intent):** Authored exclusively by the Owner or Leader. This defines the "What" and "Why" of the project.
*   **The Heritage Pulse (Timeline):** A collaborative record. Once a Master (L1) has endorsed the project, the Timeline becomes **Immutable** (Add-only).
*   **Intellectual Assets (Case Studies):** Every member is the sovereign author of their own Case Study. 
    *   **AI Smart Drafting:** AI assists in structuring raw technical data into a high-fidelity narrative but never replaces the expert's voice.

### 3. Interaction & Reputation Engine
*   **Endorse Heritage (The Vote):**
    *   **Gatekeeping:** Only members with **Level 1-3** status can "Endorse" (Vote).
    *   **Visibility:** Endorsements are public and weigh heavily on the project's **Heritage Capital ($R$)**.
*   **Visibility Tracking:** Views from **Enterprise Accounts** are tracked separately to measure "Market Resonance."
*   **Expert Insights (Professional Comments):**
    *   1HUB does not have a "Social Comment Section." 
    *   It features **Expert Insights**, restricted to members of a certain Level to ensure a high-signal environment.

### 4. The Creator's Charter (How it works)
When a project is initiated, the following flow is enforced:
1.  **Initiation:** Owner creates the record and invites the synergy team.
2.  **Validation:** Members accept roles and verify their individual Timelines.
3.  **Cultivation:** Each expert authors their Case Study demonstrating their unique professional logic.
4.  **Consecration:** The Master community audits and Endorses the project to finalize it as a **Global Heritage**.
